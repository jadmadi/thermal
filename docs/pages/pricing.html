Common questions

Does this replace the AGPL?

No. The community edition remains AGPL-3.0. This license is a separate commercial grant for internal use. It does not remove any AGPL rights.

Is this an enterprise or OEM license?

No. It covers internal commercial use only. Embedding, redistribution, hosted services, and support commitments require separate Enterprise & OEM terms.

Is support included?

Community support through GitHub Issues only. There is no SLA, uptime commitment, or priority response in the $30 license.

Is it per developer or per company?

Per named developer, for 12 months. Teams buy one seat per developer who uses Thermal.

Can I get an invoice?

Yes. Email contact@jadmadi.net with the legal entity, developer count, and billing contact.
